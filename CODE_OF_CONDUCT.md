# Code of conduct

LifeHarness is about helping people live the week they want. Its community should feel the same way: welcoming, patient and respectful, whoever you are and wherever you come from.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent. Many people here are new to open source.
- Disagree with ideas, never with people. Explain your reasons.
- Respect people's privacy. Never ask for, or share, someone's personal details, plans or goals.
- Respect different beliefs and ways of living. The app supports them, and so do we.

## Not accepted

Harassment, insults, discrimination, sexual language or imagery, threats, and publishing anyone's private information.

## If something goes wrong

Report a comment, issue or discussion with GitHub's **Report content** menu (⋯ on the item), or contact the maintainer privately through **Security → Report a vulnerability** and say it's about conduct. Reports are handled confidentially. Anyone who breaks this code may be warned, have content removed, or be blocked from the project.
